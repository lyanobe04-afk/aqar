High Priority
Prompt scheduling required (< 12 hrs). HVAC degradation or safety hardware fault.
Medium Priority
Standard operational repair (< 48 hrs). Fixtures, lighting, minor plumbing.
Low Priority
Routine upkeep and cosmetic works scheduled in weekly batches.

STAGES 1–6
1
Request Submitted Logged

Tenant inquiry or on-site staff inspection logged into registry.

2
Manager Review & Triage Current Phase

Priority evaluation, emergency water/electric isolation, and booking restriction check.

3
Assigned Pending

Allocation to internal maintenance technician or approved external contractor pool.

4
In Progress Pending

On-site diagnostic, parts procurement, and physical execution of repairs.

5
Work Completed Pending

Technician completion certification and photo proof upload.

6
Manager Sign-off & Restriction Release Final Stage

Post-repair inspection, release of booking hold, and formal closing of work ticket.
